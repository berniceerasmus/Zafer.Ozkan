📐 Grammar at a glance

Should

should/shouldn't + verb

Advice and recommendations.

You should check the expiry date.

Must

must/mustn't + verb

Strong obligation or rules.

Staff must wear gloves in the lab.

Can

can/can't + verb

Ability or permission.

This device can monitor heart rate.

Have to

have to/has to + verb

Obligation from outside (rules, jobs).

I have to update the client list weekly.

Reading

Read the passage and answer the comprehension questions

A1–A2

Read the passage below carefully. Then answer the questions in full English sentences.

Zafer often trains clinic staff on how to use new medical devices safely. He has to explain what staff should and shouldn't do, and what the rules are for each product.

For example, one new device must be stored in a cool place, and staff mustn't use it near water. It can measure results in seconds, but nurses should always calibrate it before first use.

Every month, Zafer has to review updated safety guidelines because rules change often. He also has to write a short training report after every visit.

1. What does Zafer often do for clinic staff?

2. What does he have to explain?

3. How should the new device be stored?

4. What mustn't staff do with the device?

5. What can the device do in seconds?

6. What should nurses always do before first use?

7. Why does Zafer have to review guidelines every month?

8. What else does he have to write?

9. Who is the training aimed at?

10. Find one sentence with 'must' and one with 'should' in the text.
