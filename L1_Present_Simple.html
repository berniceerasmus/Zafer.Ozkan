📐 Grammar at a glance

Affirmative

Subject + verb(+s/es)

Facts, routines, habits, jobs.

I visit clients every week. / He works in sales.

Negative

don't / doesn't + verb

Things that are not true or don't happen.

I don't work on Sundays. / She doesn't call on Fridays.

Question

Do/Does + subject + verb?

Asking about routines, habits, facts.

Do you visit hospitals often? / Does he speak English?

He / She / It

verb + -s / -es

Third person singular always adds -s or -es.

She works. He watches. It costs.

Reading

Read the passage and answer the comprehension questions

A1–A2

Read the passage below carefully. Then answer the questions in full English sentences.

Zafer works as a sales representative for a medical company in Istanbul. He usually starts work at eight o'clock and checks his emails first. Every day, he visits two or three hospitals or clinics to talk to doctors about new products.

Zafer doesn't work on weekends, but he often calls clients on Friday afternoons to confirm next week's appointments. He always prepares before a meeting, so he never forgets important information about a product.

His company sells medical equipment and medicine to hospitals across the country. Every Monday, the sales team has a meeting to talk about the week's goals. Zafer likes his job because he meets new people every day and helps doctors find the right products.

Zafer doesn't drive to work; he takes the metro because the traffic in Istanbul is often very bad. The journey usually takes about forty minutes. He always buys a coffee near the station before he gets on the train. At lunchtime, he doesn't go home — he usually eats a quick sandwich between appointments or has lunch with a doctor at the hospital cafeteria.

Zafer speaks English and Turkish fluently, but he doesn't speak French, so he sometimes has trouble when foreign colleagues visit. He doesn't like too much paperwork, so he always tries to finish his reports quickly in the evening. He never misses a deadline, and his manager trusts him with important clients.

Zafer's company doesn't only sell in Istanbul; it also sells products in Ankara, Izmir, and several smaller cities. Every three months, the company holds a conference where sales representatives from different regions share their results. Zafer usually gives a short presentation about his region because he has the highest sales numbers in his team.

After work, Zafer doesn't stay in the office late; he usually leaves by six o'clock. He plays football with friends every Wednesday evening, and on Sundays, he relaxes at home with his family. He doesn't smoke, and he tries to exercise three times a week because his job involves a lot of sitting and driving between appointments.

1. What is Zafer's job?

2. What time does he usually start work?

3. What does he do first every day?

4. How many hospitals or clinics does he usually visit each day?

5. Does Zafer work on weekends?

6. What does he often do on Friday afternoons?

7. Does he prepare before meetings? How do you know?

8. What does his company sell?

9. When does the sales team have a meeting?

10. Why does Zafer like his job?
