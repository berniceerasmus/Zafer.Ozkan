📐 Grammar at a glance

Past Simple

verb+ed / irregular form

Finished actions at a specific time in the past.

I visited the clinic yesterday. / She called last week.

Going to

am/is/are + going to + verb

Plans and intentions decided before now.

I'm going to visit three clients tomorrow.

Will

will + verb

Predictions, promises, decisions made now.

I think we will sell more next quarter. / I'll call you back.

Reading

Read the passage and answer the comprehension questions

A1–A2

Read the passage below carefully. Then answer the questions in full English sentences.

Last month, Zafer visited eight clinics and two hospitals. He didn't have any problems with deliveries, and most of his clients ordered new products. He also went to a medical conference in Ankara, where he met several new doctors.

Next month, Zafer is going to visit even more clients. He is going to launch a new product at three hospitals, and he will present it at a big conference too. His manager thinks sales will increase because the new product is very popular.

Zafer usually plans his visits one week in advance. He always checks last month's results before he decides what he is going to do next.

1. How many clinics did Zafer visit last month?

2. Did he have problems with deliveries?

3. What did most of his clients do?

4. Where did he go for a conference?

5. What is Zafer going to do next month?

6. Where is he going to launch the new product?

7. What does his manager think will happen to sales?

8. How far in advance does Zafer usually plan his visits?

9. What does he always check before planning?

10. Find one past simple and one 'going to' sentence in the text.

✓ Suggested answers

  1. He visited eight clinics.
  2. No, he didn't have any problems.
  3. Most of them ordered new products.
  4. He went to a conference in Ankara.
  5. He is going to visit even more clients.
  6. He is going to launch it at three hospitals.
  7. His manager thinks sales will increase.
  8. One week in advance.
  9. He checks last month's results.
  10. e.g. 'Zafer visited eight clinics.' / 'He is going to visit even more clients.'

Writing

A1–A2

Choose at least 4 prompts and write 1-3 sentences for each.

1. Write two sentences about what you did at work last week.
2. Write a sentence about a client visit that happened in the past.

4. Write a prediction about your sales this month using 'will'.
5. Write a sentence about something your company is going to launch.

7. Write a promise using 'will' (e.g. I will send...).
8. Write a question asking a colleague about their plans.
9. Write a negative past simple sentence about last month.
10. Write a short paragraph (3-4 sentences) about last month and next month.
