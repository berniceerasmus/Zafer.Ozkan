Lesson 5: Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Grammar: Present Simple vs Present Continuous / Genis Zaman ve Simdiki Zaman

Present SimplePresent Continuous
Useroutines, facts, habitsnow, around now, future arrangements
FormI work / she worksI am working / she is working
NegativeI don't work / she doesn't workI am not working / she isn't working
QuestionDo you work? / Does she work?Are you working? / Is she working?
Time wordsevery day, usually, often, on Mondaysnow, at the moment, today, this week, tomorrow

-ing spelling: work - working / make - making (drop e) / run - running (double) / study - studying.

Not used with -ing (state verbs): know, like, want, need, understand. Say: I know (not I am knowing).

TR: Present simple = "Her gun calisirim" (aliskanlik, gercek). Present continuous = "Su an calisiyorum" / "Yarin toplanti yapiyorum" (simdi, bu hafta, planli gelecek). Dikkat: want, know, like ile -ing kullanma.

Homework: use the grammar box in the Lesson tab if you need help. Do it alone, then press Check.
Teacher only. Plan, tips and all answers for Lesson 5 and its homework.

45-minute plan

  • Warm-up irregular verbs 5 min (new: give, take, tell, write, meet).
  • Grammar box 5 min. Elicit first: mime "I am writing / I am drinking" then ask "What do you do every day?" to show the contrast.
  • Gaps 4, Match 3, Errors 5, Word order 5, Dialogue 4, Reading 8, Discussion 6 min. Writing: start in class, finish at home.

Tips and common mistakes

  • Turkish interference: Turkish "-iyor" covers both now and routine ("Calisiyorum"), so Zafer often uses present continuous for routines. Anchor on time words: every day/usually = simple; now/at the moment = continuous.
  • State verbs: want, know, like, need, understand. He may say "I am wanting". Give a short list to copy.
  • Mixing auxiliaries: "Do you are working?" Say: do/does belongs to simple, am/is/are belongs to continuous. Never together.
  • Spelling: run - running, make - making, study - studying.
  • Future arrangements: "I am meeting the manager tomorrow" is a plan with a time. Link to Lesson 3 (going to) and say both are possible.
  • Speaking: he is A2 for speaking, so accept short answers and push for the second sentence with "because" or a time word. Correct only tense choice.
  • Match, dialogue and reading answers are scrambled. At the end, have him read the phone call aloud with you as Murat.
  • Scoring: 65 auto-checked points (warm-up 5, then 6 sections of 10). Discussion and writing are teacher assessed.

Homework notes

  • Set the Homework tab at the end of the lesson (about 45 minutes, same scoring: 65 auto-checked points). He does it alone, with no teacher help, and you review his score and the hints he needed at the start of the next lesson.
  • Same structure as the lesson, new sentences, so he practises the same rule with fresh content. Recycles the lesson grammar only.
  • Check the writing and discussion yourself: ask him to read 3 writing sentences aloud and fix them together.
