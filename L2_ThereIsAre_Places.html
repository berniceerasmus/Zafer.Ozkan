There is / are & Places

Talk about stock, quantities, and locations — accurately and automatically.

A1–A2~45 minutesMedical Sales English8 sections

📐 Grammar at a glance

There is / There are

There is + singular / There are + plural

Saying what exists or is present somewhere.

There is a pharmacy near the hospital. / There are two boxes in stock.

Some / Any

some (+ / questions offering), any (- / ?)

Talking about quantities, especially uncountable or plural nouns.

We have some samples. / We don't have any samples left.

How much / How many

How much + uncountable, How many + countable

Asking about quantity.

How many boxes do you need? / How much stock do we have?

Prepositions of Place

next to, opposite, between, near, in front of

Describing where something is located.

The clinic is next to the bank. / It's opposite the pharmacy.

Reading

Read the passage and answer the comprehension questions

A1–A2

Read the passage below carefully. Then answer the questions in full English sentences.

There is a large hospital in the center of the city. Next to the hospital, there is a small pharmacy, and opposite the pharmacy, there is a car park. Many sales representatives visit this hospital every week.

Inside the hospital, there are several departments. The reception is near the main entrance, and the meeting rooms are between the reception and the cafeteria. There isn't a parking problem because there is a large car park nearby.

This week, there are not many samples left in the storage room. The manager says there are only a few boxes, so representatives need to ask how many samples they need before visiting the hospital.

1. What is in the center of the city?

2. What is next to the hospital?

3. What is opposite the pharmacy?

4. Where is the reception?

5. Where are the meeting rooms?

6. Is there a parking problem? Why or why not?

7. How many samples are left in the storage room this week?

8. What does the manager say about the boxes?

9. What should representatives ask before visiting?

10. Find one 'there is' and one 'there are' sentence in the text.

✓ Suggested answers

  1. There is a large hospital.
  2. There is a small pharmacy next to the hospital.
  3. A car park is opposite the pharmacy.
  4. The reception is near the main entrance.
  5. The meeting rooms are between the reception and the cafeteria.
  6. No, because there is a large car park nearby.
  7. Only a few boxes are left.
  8. The manager says there are only a few boxes.
  9. They should ask how many samples they need.
  10. e.g. 'There is a large hospital.' / 'There are several departments.'
