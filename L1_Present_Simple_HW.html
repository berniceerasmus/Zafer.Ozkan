📐 Grammar at a glance

Affirmative

Subject + verb(+s/es)

Facts, routines, habits, jobs.

I visit clients every week. / He works in sales.

Negative

don't / doesn't + verb

Things that are not true or don't happen.

I don't work on Sundays. / She doesn't call on Fridays.

Question

Do/Does + subject + verb?

Asking about routines, habits, facts.

Do you visit hospitals often? / Does he speak English?

Reading

Read the passage and answer the comprehension questions

A1–A2

Read the passage below carefully. Then answer the questions in full English sentences.

Elif works in the marketing department of a medical company. She usually arrives at the office at nine o'clock and checks the sales reports first. Every Tuesday, she has a meeting with the sales team to talk about new products.

Elif doesn't travel very often, but she sometimes visits other offices when the company launches a new product. She always prepares a short presentation before these visits, so she never forgets important details.

Her company doesn't sell directly to patients; it sells medicine and equipment to hospitals and clinics. Elif likes her job because she works with many different people and learns something new every week.

1. Where does Elif work?

2. What time does she usually arrive at the office?

3. What does she check first every day?

4. When does she have a meeting with the sales team?

5. Does Elif travel often?

6. What does she always do before a visit?

7. Who does her company sell products to?

8. Does the company sell directly to patients?

9. Why does Elif like her job?

10. Find one negative present simple sentence in the text.
