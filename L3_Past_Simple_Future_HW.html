📐 Grammar at a glance

Past Simple

verb+ed / irregular form

Finished actions at a specific time in the past.

I called the client yesterday. / We had a meeting last Monday.

Going to

am/is/are + going to + verb

Plans and intentions decided before now.

We are going to visit the new clinic next week.

Will

will + verb

Predictions, promises, decisions made now.

I'll send the samples tomorrow.

Reading

Read the passage and answer the comprehension questions

A1–A2

Read the passage below carefully. Then answer the questions in full English sentences.

Last week, Zafer visited six clients and signed two new contracts. He didn't have any delivery problems, and most of his meetings went well. He also called an old client to ask about a delayed order.

Next week, Zafer is going to visit two more hospitals, and he is going to present a new catalogue at both meetings. He will also call the client with the delayed order to check on the delivery. His manager thinks next month will be very successful.

Zafer always writes a short plan every Sunday evening for the week ahead, so he never forgets an important visit.

1. How many clients did Zafer visit last week?

2. How many contracts did he sign?

3. Did he have delivery problems?

4. Who did he call, and why?

5. What is Zafer going to do next week?

6. What will he present at both meetings?

7. What will he do about the delayed order?

8. What does his manager think about next month?

9. When does Zafer write his weekly plan?

10. Find one past simple and one 'going to' sentence in the text.
