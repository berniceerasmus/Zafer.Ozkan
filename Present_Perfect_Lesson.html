Present Perfect — Speaking Lesson

for · since · already · yet · just — 45 minutes

for · since · already · yet · just — 45 dakika
📖 Grammar Reference / Gramer Özeti (click to open) ▼

Structure / Yapı: S + have/has + V3

I have worked here for 3 years. → I haven't finished yet. → Have you seen him since Monday?

FOR a period of time (duration) bir süre (ne kadar zamandır) I have worked here for three years.
SINCE a starting point in time bir başlangıç noktası (ne zamandan beri) I have worked here since 2021.
ALREADY sooner than expected, mid-sentence, affirmative beklenenden önce, cümle ortasında, olumlu I have already finished the report.
YET negatives & questions, end of sentence olumsuz/soru, cümle sonunda I haven't finished it yet. Have you finished yet?
JUST very recently, mid-sentence az önce, cümle ortasında I have just sent the email.

1. Warm-up — Quick Q&A

5 min

Ask Zafer these questions and have him answer in full present perfect sentences.

How long have you worked at your company?
Şirketinde ne kadar zamandır çalışıyorsun?
Have you ever traveled to Romania?
Hiç Romanya'ya seyahat ettin mi?
Have you finished all your tasks for today yet?
Bugünkü tüm işlerini henüz bitirdin mi?

2. Fill in the Gaps — for / since / already / yet / just

10 min

Zafer types the missing word, then says the full sentence out loud. / Zafer eksik kelimeyi yazsın, sonra cümleyi sesli söylesin.

1. I have lived in this city ten years.
2. We have sent the report — you don't need to remind me.
3. Has he finished the safety training ?
4. I have worked with this supplier 2019.
5. She has left — you just missed her.
6. They haven't replied to the email .
7. I have known my manager five years.
8. Have you eaten? We can order lunch.

3. Sentence Building — Speak from prompts

15 min

Zafer builds a full spoken sentence from each prompt. Push him to add a for/since/already/yet/just where natural. / Her ipucundan tam bir cümle kursun; uygun yerde for/since/already/yet/just eklesin.

you / work / this company
you / not finish / the monthly report
your team / already / solve / the safety issue
you / ever / visit / a Romanian supplier
the shipment / just / arrive
you / not call / the client / yet

4. Free Speaking — Work Context

10 min

Open conversation. Encourage at least 5 present perfect sentences. / Serbest konuşma. En az 5 present perfect cümlesi kurdurun.

Tell me about a project you have been working on recently. What have you already done, and what haven't you finished yet?
Son zamanlarda üzerinde çalıştığın bir projeden bahset. Şimdiye kadar ne yaptın, neyi henüz bitirmedin?
Pacing: Warm-up 5 / Gap-fill 10 / Sentence building 15 / Free speaking 10 / buffer 5.
Watch for: word order (already/just before main verb, yet at end), confusing "since" with a duration ("since 3 years" is wrong — should be "for 3 years").
If he's fast: push into Present Perfect Continuous ("How long have you been working on...") as a stretch.
If he struggles: drop back to yes/no questions with have/has before asking for full sentences.
