Simple Present & Simple Past — Verb List

Zafer Ozkan · study today, test tomorrow

How to study this list: Cover the "Simple Past" column with your hand or a paper. Look at the base verb, say the past form out loud, then check if you were right. Do this for every verb, every day, until you don't need to think about it. The green verbs are regular (just add -ed). The orange verbs are irregular — they change in their own special way, so they need extra memory work.
