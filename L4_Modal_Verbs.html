Modal Verbs

Give advice, state rules, and talk about obligations — accurately and automatically.

A1–A2~45 minutesMedical Sales English8 sections

📐 Grammar at a glance

Should

should/shouldn't + verb

Advice and recommendations.

You should take this medicine with food.

Must

must/mustn't + verb

Strong obligation or rules.

Patients must read the instructions first.

Can

can/can't + verb

Ability or permission.

This product can reduce pain quickly.

Have to

have to/has to + verb

Obligation from outside (rules, jobs).

I have to visit five clients every week.

Reading

Read the passage and answer the comprehension questions

A1–A2

Read the passage below carefully. Then answer the questions in full English sentences.

Zafer's job requires him to know a lot about the products he sells. He has to explain how each product works and what patients should and shouldn't do when using it.

For example, one popular product must be taken with food, and patients mustn't take it together with certain other medicines. It can reduce symptoms quickly, but doctors should always check a patient's medical history first.

Every week, Zafer has to update his knowledge about new products because the company regularly launches new items. He also has to fill out reports about every visit, so doctors can always find the information they need.

1. What does Zafer's job require him to know?

2. What does he have to explain to doctors?

3. How should the popular product be taken?

4. What mustn't patients do with this product?

5. What can the product do quickly?

6. What should doctors always check first?

7. Why does Zafer have to update his knowledge every week?

8. What else does he have to fill out?

9. Why are the reports useful for doctors?

10. Find one sentence with 'must' and one with 'should' in the text.

✓ Suggested answers

  1. A lot about the products he sells.
  2. How each product works and what patients should/shouldn't do.
  3. It must be taken with food.
  4. They mustn't take it with certain other medicines.
  5. It can reduce symptoms quickly.
  6. A patient's medical history.
  7. Because the company regularly launches new items.
  8. Reports about every visit.
  9. So doctors can always find the information they need.
  10. e.g. 'It must be taken with food.' / 'Doctors should always check a patient's medical history first.'
